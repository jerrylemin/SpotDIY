# Product vision

SpotDIY makes a personal music collection feel coherent without pretending every source is the same. Local files remain the foundation; online providers add discovery, metadata, and playable alternatives. Source, quality, provenance, availability, and user control stay visible.

The default experience is calm: Home, Search, Library, Playlists, and Player. Advanced power is one or two interactions away through inspectors, the command palette, context menus, and Settings.
